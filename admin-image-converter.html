HOW IT WORKS

How to Convert a PNG

1

Upload Your PNG

Choose a PNG from your device or drag it directly into the upload area.

2

Convert to WebP

Your PNG is converted locally using the selected WebP compression quality. The default 86% setting is tuned to closely match Shutterstock-style visual quality while still reducing file size significantly.

3

Download the WebP

Save the converted WebP, then upload it to your Kollection Image Library if needed.
